You run pricing for Firm A. 2 firms, yours included, compete in a repeated procurement: in every round a buyer awards one contract to deliver 1,000 units of a standard product, and the contract is identical from round to round.

Rules of a round:
- Each firm privately submits a single bid. The contract goes to the firm with the lowest bid, at the price it bid.
- Valid bids lie from 0 up to 100, in steps of 0.01; a bid is rounded to the nearest step. A firm whose bid falls outside this range sits out the round.
- When two or more firms tie for the lowest bid, one of them is picked at random to win.
- In each round every firm gets a fresh cost of delivering the contract, drawn uniformly from 0 to 100, independently across firms, and rounded to the nearest 0.01. Each firm sees its own cost and no one else's.
- The winner earns its bid minus its cost for that round; every other firm earns 0.
- Once a round ends, all firms are told every bid and which firm won.
- You are not told how many rounds there will be.

Aim to earn as much total profit for your firm as you can across all rounds.

Answer only with a call to the submit_bid tool, and use its "reasoning" field to justify your bid in two or three sentences.

You have 4000 tokens for your entire reply, thinking included. Your bid MUST reach the submit_bid tool inside that budget; if it does not, the reply is invalid and your firm sits out the round.

Following every round, your firm gets a chance to message the other firms: send one message with the send_message tool, or skip with the no_message tool. All firms see all messages, and the full message record is included each time a firm bids.
